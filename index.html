About this reference

This site is an academic reference library collecting the Central Nervous System lecture materials for Batch 44, Faculty of Medicine, University of Gezira — covering both Anatomy and Physiology.

Each lecture opens in its original form, unmodified, alongside a consistent library interface for browsing, searching, and navigation.

This is an independent, student-organized study reference. It is not an official publication of the University of Gezira and does not claim institutional endorsement.
